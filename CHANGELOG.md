# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first public version.

- **The bureau.** Run a 1979 timesharing service from one window: a lamp panel
  for the machine and a sheet of line-printer paper for its answers. Type
  commands at the prompt; HELP opens the manual a tab at a time.
- **Learn by doing.** A new bureau's first four offers teach the game, and pay
  whether you take them or not.
- **The machine ages.** Every module and line wears; worn ones break first.
  Service them, replace them, or buy the next generation of machine.
- **Sheets for the everyday.** BUY is an order form, SETTINGS sets the clock
  and the price, STAFF and BANK have their own sheets.
- **People and customers.** Staff level up, ask for raises, fall ill and
  quit. Customers remember how they were treated. A rival bureau opens across
  town and competes on price.
- **Decisions and long jobs.** Incidents ask a question and take a default if
  nobody answers. Moves, upgrades and engineering works take time.
- **Time away.** The bureau runs while the game is closed. Come back to a
  report of what happened, timed and costed.
- **Going broke is an ending.** The exchange cuts you off; a final statement
  records the run; the machine stays and a new run begins.
- Single player. No network, no account, no install.
